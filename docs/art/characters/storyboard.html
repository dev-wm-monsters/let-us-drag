Let us drag! — 10 alternative characters

Concept art for a vote. Each character keeps the Uncle’s construction (bold variable-weight ink, flat warm colours, cel shading, layered rig) and has a huge mouth that opens wide to eat files. Left: idle. Right: eating, eyes up-left on the incoming file.

01

Chacha Gappu

India — everybody’s favourite neighbourhood chacha

Signature
A folded paan in his fist and a paan-red grin; the vest buttons strain over his belly.
Says
“Arre wah!”
Eats
Pats his belly, says “Arre wah!” and slurps the file in like the last gulab jamun.
Chews
Slow, happy chewing with eyes shut; the belly jiggles and a vest button pings off at 100 %.
Spits
Tucks the result into his vest pocket and hands it over with a proud “Le beta”.
02

Dadi Amma

India — the grandma who feeds everyone, including your files

Signature
Her steel pressure cooker: it whistles when the conversion is done. Cat-eye glasses on a beaded chain.
Says
“Kuch khaya ki nahi?”
Eats
Pushes her glasses up, says “Arre, so thin!” and gulps the file down to fatten it up.
Chews
Chews patiently while counting cooker whistles — one whistle per 25 %.
Spits
The cooker gives the final “phweeeet!” and she hands the result over, still warm.
03

Halwai Bhaiya

India — the sweet-shop owner at the corner, towel on the shoulder since 1987

Signature
A giant, glistening jalebi held up like a trophy (and the red-checked towel on his shoulder).
Says
“Garam garam!”
Eats
Drops the file into his mouth like batter into hot oil — “Garam garam!”.
Chews
Chews in spirals; the progress ring is drawn as a jalebi coiling itself, sizzling.
Spits
Wipes his brow with the towel and serves the result on a leaf plate.
04

Auto-wala Uncle

India (Mumbai) — the rickshaw driver who knows every shortcut

Signature
His black-and-yellow fare meter: the flag drops when he starts chewing and the fare ticks up as progress. Plus a magnificent curled moustache.
Says
“Chalo, meter se!”
Eats
Twirls his moustache, flips the meter flag down — “Chalo!” — and the file hops in like a passenger.
Chews
The meter clicks along with every chew; at 100 % it rings “ting!”.
Spits
Hands the result over with a receipt-style card and a two-finger salute.
05

Great-Uncle Nickels

International — a roadside “Mystery Deals” shack great-uncle (an original take on the lovable con-man genre)

Signature
One coin per file, flipped with a wink and a gold-tooth “ting”; a crooked toupee that lifts off when he gulps; the hand-painted MYSTERY DEALS sign.
Says
“One coin. No refunds. Ha!”
Eats
Flips a coin, bites it to check it is real, then gulps the file with the coin chaser.
Chews
Chews suspiciously, eyes darting; the sign flips from MYSTERY DEALS to ALMOST DONE.
Spits
Slaps the result on the counter: “Genuine article! Practically new!”
06

Big Bertha the Lunch Lady

USA — the school cafeteria legend with a heart of gold

Signature
Her ladle (always with a dollop of mash) and the compartment lunch tray; the hairnet over a magnificent updo.
Says
“Next! Don’t hold up the line, sugar.”
Eats
Scoops the file with her ladle — “Next!” — and slurps it straight off the ladle.
Chews
Chews like she is taste-testing the daily special, humming; the hairnet bounces.
Spits
Slops the result onto a lunch tray, one neat compartment per file: “Enjoy, sugar.”
07

Sumo-san

Japan — a gentle, very polite giant of the dohyō

Signature
His neat oiled topknot that boings on every stomp, and the navy mawashi with its hanging cords.
Says
“Itadakimasu!”
Eats
Bows a tiny bow, says “Itadakimasu!”, then claps both hands and swallows the file in one go.
Chews
Chews with a slow shiko stomp — left, right — the window shakes a little and dust puffs fly.
Spits
Presents the result with both hands and a nod: “Gochisōsama deshita.”
08

Nonna Pina

Italy — a nonna from a little town where Sunday lunch lasts until dinner

Signature
Her rolling pin and the flour dusted on her cheeks (plus a floury handprint on the apron).
Says
“Mangia, mangia!”
Eats
Dusts the file with flour, rolls it flat with her rolling pin and slurps it like a ribbon of tagliatelle.
Chews
Kneads while she chews — the progress ring fills like dough rising.
Spits
Cuts the result into neat pasta-shaped cards: “Perfetto!” with a chef’s kiss.
09

Captain Chomp

The Seven Seas — a jolly (and slightly lost) pirate captain

Signature
Polly the parrot on his shoulder squawks the format name (“PNG! PNG!”); a peg leg that taps while he waits.
Says
“Arr, feed me, matey!”
Eats
Bellows “Arr!”, catches the file like a cannonball and swallows it whole while Polly squawks the format.
Chews
Chews like he is tasting sea salt, peg leg tapping in rhythm; Polly counts “25! 50! 75!”.
Spits
Spits the result into a little treasure chest that pops open with a sparkle: “Treasure!”
10

Fridge Frank

Nowhere and everywhere — a grumpy retro fridge from a 1950s kitchen

Signature
Fridge magnets are his format buttons (PNG, JPG, PDF, MP3, WEBP); ice-cube eyes; a chrome “FRANK” badge.
Says
“Close the door, you’re letting the cold out!”
Eats
Grumbles, swings his door wide open (icicle teeth, frosty breath) and the file slides onto a shelf.
Chews
Hums like an old compressor — “mmmrrrrr” — while frost creeps up the progress ring.
Spits
Pops the result out chilled with a little cloud of frost and slams the door: “Next time knock.”
